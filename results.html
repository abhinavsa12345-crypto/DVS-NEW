Feasibility: the plan fits the field force

A targeting model is worthless if the resulting call plan cannot physically be delivered. This is tested explicitly, at both the planning and pessimistic bounds.

Published Indian benchmarks put representative productivity at 8–12 calls per day, so the assumptions used sit at the conservative end of industry practice.

Engagement and adaptation

Scores allocate resource; playbooks determine what happens during the contact; recalibration keeps the model honest.

P Platinum — 3–4 visits / month

Week 1: clinical deep dive, pivotal trial data, starter samples.
Week 2: patient experience, real-world evidence, objection handling.
Week 3: CME invitation, new publications, prescription audit.
Week 4: expand usage, recognise prescriptions written.

G Gold — 2 visits / month

Week 1: core detailing with competitive comparison, samples.
Week 3: prescribing feedback, peer case studies, digital event invitations.

S Silver — 1 visit / month

Brief reminder detailing, leave-behind literature, brand visibility maintenance, digital follow-up through approved channels.

B Bronze — digital only

No routine visits. Monthly compliant reminders, quarterly webinars, e-detailing access. A visit is triggered by an interest signal — sample request, webinar attendance or first prescription — at which point the doctor is reclassified upward.

Quarterly recalibration

Every 90 days the universe is re-scored using observed behaviour rather than pre-launch estimates. The pre-launch score is a prior; actual prescribing is evidence.

MovementTrigger
Silver → GoldConsistent prescribing established; strong engagement signal
Bronze → SilverAttended a webinar, requested samples, or wrote a first prescription
Gold → PlatinumVolume exceeds expectation; demonstrates peer influence
Platinum → GoldStopped prescribing despite sustained engagement
Gold → SilverPrescriptions declining; competitor has won the doctor back
The economic logic. A doctor scored Platinum who has written nothing in three months despite twelve contacts is, on the evidence, not a Platinum doctor. Continuing to fund that assessment is the precise error the model exists to prevent.
